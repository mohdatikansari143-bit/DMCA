1. Definitions & Interpretations

1.1 Defined Terms

For the purposes of this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below:

Platform: The Trex Wallet digital ecosystem, including mobile applications, web interfaces, APIs, and all associated services.

Trex Coin / TREX: The native utility token and digital asset native to the Trex Wallet ecosystem, operating on supported blockchain networks.

USDT: Tether (USD Tether), a stablecoin digital asset pegged to the United States Dollar, supported for conversion and exchange within the Platform.

User / You: Any natural person or legal entity that registers an account, accesses, or utilizes the Platform and its services.

Wallet: The digital custodial storage facility provided by the Platform for holding, managing, and transacting supported digital assets.

Conversion: The act of exchanging Trex Coin for USDT, or USDT for Trex Coin, at prevailing or platform-determined exchange rates.

Exchange: The service enabling Users to trade, swap, transfer, or withdraw digital assets to external addresses, third-party platforms, or fiat on-ramp/off-ramp partners.

KYC: Know Your Customer — the identity verification and due diligence process required for account activation and transaction authorization.

AML: Anti-Money Laundering — policies, procedures, and controls designed to detect and prevent money laundering and illicit financial activities.

CFT: Countering the Financing of Terrorism — measures to prevent the use of digital assets for terrorist financing.

Blockchain: The distributed ledger technology underlying the digital assets supported on the Platform.

Private Keys: Cryptographic codes that provide access to and control over digital assets; managed securely by the Platform under custodial arrangements.

Transaction: Any operation involving deposit, withdrawal, conversion, exchange, or transfer of digital assets on the Platform.

Service Fees: All charges, commissions, spreads, and network fees levied by the Platform for executing transactions and providing services.

Force Majeure: Events beyond the reasonable control of either party, including but not limited to natural disasters, wars, acts of terrorism, government actions, regulatory changes, internet failures, and blockchain network disruptions.

Business Day: Any day other than Saturday, Sunday, or a public holiday in the primary jurisdiction of Platform operations.

Fiat: Government-issued currency, such as USD, EUR, GBP, or other sovereign currencies.

Gas Fee: The transaction fee required to process operations on a blockchain network, paid to network validators or miners.

Slippage: The difference between the expected price of a conversion/exchange and the actual executed price, typically occurring in volatile or low-liquidity markets.

Cold Storage: Offline storage of digital assets using hardware security modules (HSMs) and air-gapped systems to prevent unauthorized access.

MFA: Multi-Factor Authentication — a security mechanism requiring two or more verification factors to access an account.

Sanctions List: Any list of prohibited persons, entities, or jurisdictions maintained by the United Nations, OFAC, EU, HMT, or other applicable regulatory authorities.

4. Wallet Services & Custody

4.1 Wallet Creation & Architecture

  • Upon successful registration and KYC verification, a digital Wallet will be provisioned for each supported asset.
  • Each Wallet is associated with unique blockchain addresses generated using cryptographically secure random number generation.
  • The Platform employs a hybrid custody model consisting of hot wallets for immediate transactions, warm reserves, and secure offline cold storage.

4.2 Supported Digital Assets

Trex Coin (TREX): Supported on Native Chain, BEP-20, ERC-20. Operations: Deposit (Enabled), Withdrawal (Enabled), Conversion (Enabled), Exchange (Enabled).

Tether (USDT): Supported on ERC-20, TRC-20, BEP-20, Polygon. Operations: Deposit (Enabled), Withdrawal (Enabled), Conversion (Enabled), Exchange (Enabled).

4.3 Custodial Arrangement & Key Management

  • Trex Wallet operates as a custodial wallet service.
  • Private keys are managed securely by the Platform using Multi-Party Computation (MPC) technology and Hardware Security Modules (HSMs) certified to FIPS 140-2 Level 3 standards.
  • Users retain beneficial ownership of their digital assets subject to the terms of this Agreement.
  • The Platform does not provide Users with direct access to private keys or recovery seed phrases.

4.4 Deposit Protocol

Minimum Deposit: No minimum limits for most assets; network-dependent on selected chains.

Confirmations Required: Varies by asset structure: TREX (12 confirmations), USDT-ERC20 (12 confirmations), USDT-TRC20 (19 confirmations).

Deposit Address: Unique per asset per user profile. Addresses do not expire but must be checked prior to each transaction.

Incorrect Deposits: Deposits to wrong addresses or unsupported networks will result in irreversible asset loss. The Platform charges an administrative recovery fee of 100 USDT or 5% of deposit value (whichever is higher) for manual recovery attempts, though recovery remains unguaranteed.

Memo/Tag Requirements: Certain blockchain networks require destination tags. Failure to include tags may result in a permanent loss of funds.

4.5 Withdrawal Protocol

Withdrawal Request: Submitted via the Platform interface, subject to automated compliance checks and security review.

Security Review: Withdrawals undergo automated AML screening and may trigger manual review for large amounts or suspicious patterns.

Limits and Processing: Limits are determined by active KYC tier. Transactions are typically processed within minutes but may extend to 24-48 hours for manual reviews.

Fees and Whitelisting: Network fees are deducted directly from the withdrawal amount and displayed prior to final confirmation. We highly recommend utilizing address whitelisting to bypass certain cooling holds.

5. Trex Coin ↔ USDT Conversion Engine

5.1 Conversion Mechanism

The Platform provides a seamless, real-time conversion service enabling Users to exchange Trex Coin (TREX) for Tether (USDT) and vice versa.

  • Real-Time Rates: Conversion rates are updated continuously based on market liquidity and platform algorithms.
  • Rate Lock: Upon User confirmation, the conversion rate is locked for 30 seconds to prevent slippage during volatile market conditions.
  • Instant Settlement: Conversions are typically executed within seconds and credited immediately to the User's Wallet.
  • Transparent Pricing: All fees, spreads, and net amounts are displayed clearly before confirmation.

5.2 Conversion Process Flow

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
                    TREX -> USDT CONVERSION STEPS
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. User initiates conversion in dashboard.
  2. Platform displays the current exchange rate, fee deduction, and net yield.
  3. User confirms transaction and inputs MFA parameters.
  4. Platform locks rate for 30 seconds and processes internal ledger states.
  5. Balance is updated: TREX balance is deducted and USDT is credited instantly.
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------

5.3 Conversion Rate Determination Factors

Market Liquidity: Rates are influenced by available liquidity in the Platform's internal pools and external market makers.

Platform Spread: A spread of 0.5% to 2.0% may be applied to the mid-market rate to cover operational costs and market risk.

Volatility Adjustment: During periods of high volatility, additional spread or temporary suspension may be applied to protect Users and the Platform.

Rate Lock: Once confirmed by the User, the rate is locked for 30 seconds. If execution fails within this window, the User must reconfirm at the updated rate.

5.4 Conversion Fee Schedule

Platform Conversion Fee: 0.5% – 2.0% per transaction (Deducted from the output amount. Rate varies by KYC tier, volume, and market conditions).

Network / Gas Fee: Variable (market-dependent fee paid to validators/miners for transaction processing).

Minimum Fee: Equivalent to 1.00 USDT (Applied if the calculated fee falls below this threshold).

Maximum Fee: No maximum cap (For extremely large transactions, fees are calculated on a percentage basis without upper limit).

5.5 Conversion Limits

Minimum Conversion: 10 USDT equivalent per transaction. (Applies to both TREX→USDT and USDT→TREX).

Maximum Conversion (Tier 1): 1,000 USDT per day.

Maximum Conversion (Tier 2): 10,000 USDT per day.

Maximum Conversion (Tier 3): 100,000 USDT per day.
