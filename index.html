4. Wallet Services & Custody

4.1 Wallet Creation & Architecture

  • Upon successful registration and KYC verification, a digital Wallet will be provisioned for each supported asset.
  • Each Wallet is associated with unique blockchain addresses generated using cryptographically secure random number generation.
  • The Platform employs a hybrid custody model:
    • Hot Wallets: Small percentage of assets for immediate liquidity and transaction processing.
    • Warm Wallets: Medium-security storage for operational reserves.
    • Cold Storage: Majority of assets stored in offline, air-gapped hardware security modules (HSMs) with multi-signature requirements.

4.3 Custodial Arrangement & Key Management

  • Trex Wallet operates as a custodial wallet service.
  • Private keys are managed by the Platform using:
    • Multi-Party Computation (MPC) technology.
    • Hardware Security Modules (HSMs) certified to FIPS 140-2 Level 3 or higher.
    • Shamir's Secret Sharing for distributed key management.
    • Geographic distribution of key shards across secure data centers.
  • Users retain beneficial ownership of their digital assets subject to the terms of this Agreement.
  • The Platform does NOT provide Users with direct access to private keys or seed phrases.

5. Trex Coin ↔ USDT Conversion Engine

5.1 Conversion Mechanism

The Platform provides a seamless, real-time conversion service enabling Users to exchange Trex Coin (TREX) for Tether (USDT) and vice versa.

Key Features:

  • Real-Time Rates: Conversion rates are updated continuously based on market liquidity and platform algorithms.
  • Rate Lock: Upon User confirmation, the conversion rate is locked for 30 seconds to prevent slippage during volatile market conditions.
  • Instant Settlement: Conversions are typically executed within seconds and credited immediately to the User's Wallet.
  • Transparent Pricing: All fees, spreads, and net amounts are displayed clearly before confirmation.
