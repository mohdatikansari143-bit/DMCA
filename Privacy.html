6. Blockchain Data and Immutability

IMPORTANT NOTICE REGARDING BLOCKCHAIN TECHNOLOGY:

The Services interact with public blockchain networks. When you initiate a transaction involving Trex Coin, USDT, or other digital assets, the transaction details (including wallet addresses, amounts, and timestamps) are permanently recorded on the respective blockchain ledger.

Due to the immutable and decentralized nature of blockchain technology:

  • We cannot delete, modify, or erase transaction records from public blockchains
  • Your wallet address and transaction history remain publicly visible even after account deletion
  • Blockchain data exists independently of our Services and servers
  • Privacy features of public blockchains are limited by their underlying protocols

By using our Services, you acknowledge and accept these inherent characteristics of blockchain technology.
