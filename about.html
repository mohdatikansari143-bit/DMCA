4. Custodial Digital Wallet Services & Asset Management

4.1 Trex Wallet provides secure custodial storage for Trex Coin, USDT, and other supported cryptocurrencies, utilizing advanced multi-party computation (MPC) systems coupled with high-security offline vaults.

4.2 We maintain isolated, multisig cold storage facilities to protect funds from malicious online incursions. These wallets require multiple validation signatures from distributed geographic keys before any outward transfer is authorized.

4.3 The system provides continuous balance tracking, real-time transaction history logs, and instant conversion capabilities through our integrated ledger system.

4.4 Our backend systems maintain a strict 1:1 reserve ratio for all user-deposited digital assets. This ensures that every unit of virtual currency displayed in your dashboard is fully backed by corresponding assets held securely in cold and warm custody environments.

4.5 Users do not have direct control over the physical private keys of the custodial wallet files. Instead, keys are stored dynamically as encrypted shards distributed across multiple enterprise-grade cloud security modules.

4.6 Any outward transaction must pass automated, programmatic security checks designed to detect wallet tampering, balance manipulation, or unverified ledger state changes before it is pushed to the public blockchain network.

5. Trex Coin to USDT Conversion Framework

5.1 The system features a native conversion mechanism enabling real-time transactions between Trex Coin and Tether (USDT). Conversions are executed dynamically based on the current algorithmic valuation model configured in the core ledger.

5.2 Once a conversion command is confirmed by the user within the interface, the transaction is logged as final. Conversions cannot be cancelled, reversed, or refunded under any circumstances due to the immutable nature of the blockchain ledgers with which we interact.

5.3 Conversions are processed instantly on our internal off-chain ledger, and updated balances are reflected in your account dashboard immediately upon successful state validation.

5.4 The platform relies on aggregate market oracle feeds to determine precise exchange rates. We take no responsibility for conversion latency or rate adjustments caused by network disconnects or extreme market disruptions on external third-party platforms.

5.5 To protect the platform ecosystem from algorithmic failure, we enforce minimum and maximum conversion amounts per transaction. These thresholds adjust dynamically based on market liquidity and token price volatility.

5.6 In the event of an obvious technical error or feed glitch that results in an incorrect conversion rate being processed, the Company reserves the absolute right to correct the transaction record and adjust the user's balances accordingly.

7. Transparent Fee Structures & Conversion Rate Dynamics

7.1 All conversions, withdrawals, and premium network transfers are subject to clearly visible administrative and blockchain network gas fees. These fees are dynamically calculated at the precise moment of transaction initiation.

7.2 Market volatility can trigger immediate adjustments in conversion rates. The rate shown in the portal is locked for a maximum of 15 seconds. If the user fails to authorize the execution within this window, the rate updates automatically.

7.3 Our administrative fees are deducted directly from the final transacted asset. This ensures that users do not need to maintain separate gas token balances to perform conversions or withdrawals inside the system.

7.4 We maintain full fee transparency. A detailed breakdown of the applicable network fee, platform service fee, and conversion spread is presented to the user before they click the final transaction confirmation button.

7.5 Any changes to our base fee structure or administrative rates will be communicated to the user base via our official channel dashboards at least 7 business days prior to implementation.

8.4 Daily withdrawal counters reset automatically at 00:00 Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) each day. Any unutilized daily withdrawal limit cannot be rolled over or added to the following day's limits.
